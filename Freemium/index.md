# Freemium Software

This section tracks freemium tools that people in the org have actually used or evaluated and found acceptable.

Entries here can be free tier, freemium, or limited free offers. Notes are practical observations from use, not marketing claims.

If you have something useful to add, contribute it with short real-world notes so others can make a quick call.

## What You Will Find

- Tools people here have actually run, tested, or reviewed
- Limits that matter in practice
- Tradeoffs that show up after setup, not just at signup

## Contribution Notes

- Keep entries concise and technical
- Prefer tested behavior over promotional language
- Include gotchas, limits, and small costs when relevant
